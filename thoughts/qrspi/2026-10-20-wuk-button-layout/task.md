# Task

Rework how WarlockUtilityKit (WUK) arranges its buttons — the healthstone/soulstone/etc. stone buttons, armor and pet prep buttons, and related labels — so they are no longer a simple horizontal bar (`WUKStrip`). The goal is a more themed, interesting and dynamic arrangement (e.g. a radial/ring, pentagram, orbit, or state-driven expanding layout) that still works with secure action buttons, saved positions, and docking.
