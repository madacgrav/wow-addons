# Research Questions

## Context
Focus on WarlockUtilityKit (`Summon.lua`, `Comm.lua`, `Trade.lua`, `Board.lua`, `Core.lua`), ProfessionPlanner (`Core.lua`, `Logic.lua`, `Data.lua`), the `probe-results.md` files under `thoughts/qrspi/`, and the root `WoW Forever Addon Ideas — PRD.md` (Ideas 5 and 6, and the platform-constraints table). Some questions need web research into the community addon ecosystem and the WoW Forever client API (Interface 16001, Midnight "secrets").

## Questions
1. What community addons exist (CurseForge, Wago, GitHub, WoWInterface) for WoW Forever, classic-era, or retail that scan public chat for trade/service advertisements, keep a list of advertisers, broadcast a player's own professions/recipes or services (summons, portals) to other addon users, or let players mark themselves available/busy for crafting? Examples to check include Craft/LFC-style crafting directories, TradeSkillMaster's crafting features, "Trade Chat filter"/"LFG chat parser" addons, Guild Self Forever 2.0, GuildStock, and recipe-sharing addons like Skillet/TradeSkillInfo/"Recipe Radar"-style tools. Which ones load on Interface 16001, and what does each cover or leave out?
2. How does `WarlockUtilityKit/Summon.lua` parse chat today: which `CHAT_MSG_*` events it registers, how it matches text (keywords, patterns, normalization), how it extracts and normalizes the sender name (realm handling), how it stores and expires entries, and how `Trade.lua` handles incoming whispers and sends whispers back?
3. What does the WoW Forever client expose for public channel chat — `CHAT_MSG_CHANNEL` argument layout, channel numbering/names for Trade, General, LookingForGroup, Services or other Forever-specific channels, whether message text or sender name can be secret (`issecretvalue`) in any context, and any throttles or restrictions on reading channels or sending whispers? What do the repo's probe results and the PRD constraints table already record about this?
4. How does `WarlockUtilityKit/Comm.lua` send and receive addon messages: prefix registration, channel choice (GUILD/PARTY/RAID/WHISPER), the send queue and backoff, message size limits and chunking, handling of `C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage` result codes, and what is documented about trial accounts being unable to send addon messages or about addon messages over WHISPER to non-guild players on this client?
5. How does ProfessionPlanner read the player's professions and known recipes via `C_TradeSkillUI` (and related APIs): which calls, when the data is available (window open vs. any time), how recipe/skill-line IDs and names are represented, and what is persisted in SavedVariables? Is there any existing compact encoding of a recipe list in the repo?
6. How is per-character and account-wide state stored across the existing addons (SavedVariables layout, DB init, the ForeverSVFix workaround noted in the PRD), and how do they build list-style UI windows with rows, filters, and one-click actions (e.g. `Board.lua`, `Panel.lua`)?
7. What summon- and teleport/portal-related spells and items exist on WoW Forever for warlocks and mages (Ritual of Summoning, Portal/Teleport spell IDs, Meeting Stones or any Forever-specific summon or travel services), and which common phrasings do players use when advertising these services and profession crafts (e.g. "WTS", "LF enchanter", "summons", "ports to")?
