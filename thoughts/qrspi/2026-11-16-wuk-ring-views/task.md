# Task

Add spell tooltips to every icon on the Warlock Utility Kit (WUK) ring/strip, and make the soulstone icon a view toggle that cycles the ring through three views: **Party view** (the current ring — default and fallback), **Buff view** (self/party buffs such as Detect Invisibility, Demon Armor/Skin, Shadow Ward, Soul Link), and **Demon view** (all demon summons). Each click on the soulstone icon changes its color and "flips" the ring to the next view in order Party → Buff → Demon → Party.

Why: the warlock wants quick access to buffs and every demon summon from the same compact ring without extra action bars, and needs tooltips to tell the icons apart.
